TEPLO MAIL

Privacy, in plain words.

Effective 17 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the Teplo Mail Windows application and this product website. The Teplo Mail project is responsible for its own handling of data. Contact: teplomailsupport@gmail.com.

Data you choose to use

The desktop app processes imported contact email addresses, names and companies; message subjects and bodies; attachments; drafts; sending settings; delivery results; and contact exclusion preferences. These are stored in a local database and local files on your computer.

Connecting Google

You sign in on Google’s own page. Teplo Mail does not ask for or store your Google password. Basic connection requests Gmail sending permission (gmail.send) and account identity (openid and email) to identify the connected sender. Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted locally using the operating system secure-storage mechanism. Sending transmits the chosen recipients, message and attachments to Google.

Optional reply checking

Reply checking is separate and off by default. Enabling it requires your permission for gmail.readonly, which technically grants read access to Gmail. The app uses that permission to inspect conversations sent through Teplo Mail, for up to 30 days after sending, to detect replies and explicit stop requests. It stores processing cursors and limited reply records locally; it does not copy the whole mailbox into its database. Google may return message content needed to check a tracked conversation.

How Google data is used

Google data is used to send messages you authorise, identify the sender, show delivery results, and, only when enabled, check replies. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. The app does not upload Gmail data to a Teplo Mail operator server. Teplo Mail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and providers

Messages and attachments are shared with Google and the recipients you select. Google processes data under its own policies. This website’s hosting provider may process network request metadata, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. The hosting provider may set essential security cookies. We add no advertising or analytics trackers. The website only stores a theme preference locally in your browser. If you contact support, your email address and the information you send are processed to respond to your request.

Retention, deletion and backups

Desktop data remains on your computer until you remove it. Uninstalling the app preserves local data by design. Use the app’s data-folder setting to locate and remove the local data after closing the app. Contact-list deletion keeps delivery history and the separate list of excluded addresses. Backups and exported files remain wherever you save them and are not separately encrypted by the app; backups exclude Google credentials. Remove copies separately. Deleting local data does not delete messages already in Gmail or recipients’ mailboxes. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email.

Your controls

You can disconnect Gmail in the app and revoke Teplo Mail access in your Google Account connections settings. If a revocation request fails, remove access directly in Google. Turn off reply checking in Settings to stop checking; you may also revoke Google access and reconnect with sending-only permission. You can edit or delete local contacts and drafts, manage exclusions and remove your local data and backups.

Security and changes

The app uses HTTPS for Google API requests and operating-system encryption for saved tokens. Local databases, attachments and exported backups are not automatically encrypted by Teplo Mail; protect your computer and backup locations. No system guarantees absolute security. We will update this policy when the product’s data handling changes and revise the effective date.
